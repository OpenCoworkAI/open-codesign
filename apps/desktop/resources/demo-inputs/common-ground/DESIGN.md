# Common Ground Design System

## Overview
An original fictional neighborhood workshop identity by Open CoDesign
contributors, MIT licensed. Not affiliated with a real venue or organization.

## Colors
Canvas #FAF3E7; surface #FFFDF7; ink #372B25; muted ink #69574A;
terracotta accent #A8432B; leaf accent #42644A; decorative ochre #E3B75D.
Dark ink on ochre; light surface text on terracotta. Preserve contrast and pair
availability colors with explicit labels.

## Type and shape
System serif headings, system sans-serif body. Body 16px with 1.5 line height.
8px spacing rhythm, gently rounded 12px cards, generous 44px touch targets.
Original arch logo evokes shared tables; poster shapes evoke clay and leaves.

## Composition
Editorial discovery grid, a quieter details page, a focused booking form and a
clear confirmation summary. Mobile stacks content; desktop pairs practical
information with artwork. Reuse the same buttons, fields and status labels.

## Voice
Welcoming and practical. State price, duration, materials and capacity honestly.
Always distinguish a simulated reservation from a real booking.
