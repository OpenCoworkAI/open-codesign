# Design quality guard

Judge quality by fit and execution, not font or palette blacklists. A white surface, familiar typeface, standard navigation, or card grid can be appropriate to the brief. Avoid repeating a stock composition regardless of content.

Make hierarchy, alignment, contrast, and spacing intentional. Use authentic content structure and a clear primary action. Decorative imagery, motion, charts, and metrics should serve meaning. Replace generic filler with useful sample content, not invented proof. Prefer one well-resolved visual idea over competing effects; respect reduced motion and readability.
